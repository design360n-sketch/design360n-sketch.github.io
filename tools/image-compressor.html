Questions about image compressor

Is this image compressor really free?

Yes. Free, no signup, no watermark. Your photo never leaves your device.

How much smaller will my photo get?

It depends on the photo and the quality you pick. The tool shows the original and new size, and the percentage saved, before you download.

Will compressing ruin my photo quality?

At quality 80 most photos look the same and are much smaller. Go lower only when a tiny file matters more than perfect quality.
