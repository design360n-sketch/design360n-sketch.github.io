Questions about background remover

Is this background remover really free?

Yes. Free, no signup, no watermark. The photo stays on your device.

What is the difference between AI and plain-background mode?

AI mode cuts out the subject from any photo. Plain-background mode is faster and works best when the background is one solid colour.
