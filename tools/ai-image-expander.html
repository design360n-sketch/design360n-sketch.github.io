Questions about ai image expander

Is AI Expand really free?

Yes — 3 free AI uses a day across the AI tools (upscale + expand), with your own free Gemini API key (no card needed). A paid license key gives unlimited AI use.

Will my original photo change?

No — Gemini keeps your original photo as it is and only fills in the new area around it. Small details at the edges can shift slightly.

How big is the expanded photo?

Gemini returns about 1024px on the long side, so AI Expand is best for small and medium photos.

What key do I need?

A free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey). It stays only in your browser — Design360n never sees it.
