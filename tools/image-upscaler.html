Questions about image upscaler

Is this image upscaler really free?

Yes. Fast mode is free with no signup and no watermark. AI mode uses your own free Gemini API key — Google’s free tier allows about 500 images a day, no card needed.

How does the free AI upscale work?

Choose AI mode, get a free key from Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey) and save it in your browser. The key never goes to Design360n — it stays in your browser and is only sent to Google’s Gemini API with your photo.

How big is the AI result?

Gemini (Nano Banana) returns about 1024px on the long side, so AI mode is best for small photos. For big photos use Fast mode (2x / 4x).

Will upscaling make my photo sharper?

Fast mode makes the photo bigger with smooth high-quality resizing, but it cannot add detail that was never in the original photo. AI mode regenerates the image with Gemini, so it can add detail — and it can also change small details.

Does the AI result have a watermark?

Google adds an invisible SynthID watermark to AI-generated and AI-edited images. You cannot see it — it only marks the image as AI-made.
